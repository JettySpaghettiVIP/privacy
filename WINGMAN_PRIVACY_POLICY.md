# Wingman Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 9, 2026

Wingman is a paper-airplane arcade game for Android, published by **JettySpaghettiVIP**
("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app handles.

## The short version

Wingman is a fully offline game. It has no account system, no advertising, no analytics,
no telemetry, and no crash-reporting service, and it makes no network requests of any kind.
Nothing you do in the app is ever sent anywhere.

## Information the app handles

### On the device only

Wingman stores gameplay information locally on your device using Android's DataStore,
including:

- your best score, lifetime stats, and recent run history;
- unlocked aircraft, upgrades, and cosmetic trails, and your in-game currency (Stars, Ace Tokens);
- Daily Challenge progress, streaks, and a local leaderboard of your own past runs;
- app preferences (sound, haptics, reduced motion, tutorial visibility).

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and no one else can either. You can
erase it at any time using the in-app "Reset Data" option, or by clearing the app's storage
or uninstalling it.

### Permissions

Wingman requests only the Android VIBRATE permission, used for haptic feedback during
gameplay. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or any other
permission.

### Network access

Wingman makes zero network requests. It does not contact any server, ours or anyone else's —
not for ads, not for analytics, not for updates, not for anything.

## Android backup

Android's device backup and device-to-device transfer features may include Wingman's saved
gameplay data as part of your device's normal backup, according to your own Android backup
settings and Google account. This is standard Android OS behavior for app data and isn't
something Wingman does itself. You control this through your device's Settings, not through
the app.

## Sharing

We don't share, sell, or transmit your information, because the app never sends it anywhere
in the first place. There is no developer backend or server that stores Wingman user data.

## Children

Wingman is a general-audience casual arcade game suitable for players of all ages, including
children. Because the app collects no information from anyone, of any age, there is nothing
in Wingman that requires special handling for younger players — the same "nothing leaves the
device" guarantee applies to every player.

## Security and retention

Since no data leaves the device, there is no server-side security posture to describe.
On-device data remains until you clear it, reset it in-app, clear the app's storage, or
uninstall the app.

## Changes

We may update this policy if the app's data handling ever changes. The effective date above
will be updated when that happens.
