LIBERTY Backup

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

LIBERTY Backup is operated for the personal use of the owner of thumbinollie@gmail.com.

Google account access

The tool uses Google authorization to access the owner's Google Drive. Its requested permission allows access to Drive files. The intended backup workflow restricts its operations to the owner's approved backup destination; that workflow restriction does not narrow Google's account-wide permission.

The tool reads account identity and storage capacity to confirm the destination and available space. It reads backup file metadata and, when verifying or restoring backups, backup content. It creates and maintains backups only under the owner's approved backup policy.

Encryption and storage

The intended backup workflow uses rclone encryption on the private server before transmission. Backup contents, filenames, and folder names are encrypted in Google Drive. File sizes and modification times may remain visible to Google. Local backup copies may be unencrypted and are controlled separately.

Authorization credentials and encryption recovery information are kept privately by the owner. Encryption recovery information is to be retained in the owner's password manager and as a printed copy in the owner's safe.

Use and sharing

Google account data is used for backup, verification, recovery, and operational troubleshooting. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models. Encrypted backup data is transmitted to Google Drive as the selected storage provider.

Retention and control

The proposed policy keeps one current successful weekly backup and two archived successful weekly backups at each approved destination. This policy has not yet been activated. Existing files are not deleted merely by publishing this policy; approved transition and retention rules govern deletion.

The owner can stop the tool and revoke its access through the Google account's security settings. Revoking access does not itself delete backups already stored in Google Drive.
